GORILLA TAG (GTAG) DETAILED BREAKDOWN:

1. CORE GAME ENGINE & INFRASTRUCTURE
- Engine: Built entirely in Unity, specifically utilizing the XR Interaction Toolkit for VR hand input.
- Build Type: Uses Unity's IL2CPP (Intermediate Language to C++) backend for mobile optimization and performance.
- Networking: Powered by Photon Unity Networking (PUN2) and Photon Voice for proximity audio synchronization.
- Targeted Hardware: Runs natively on mobile Android VR systems (Meta Quest 1, 2, 3, Pro) at 90Hz to 120Hz, as well as PCVR via Steam.

2. THE "LEGLESS" PHYSICS ENGINE (GORILLA LOCOMOTION)
- The character avatar is just a floating primate head and torso with two arms. Virtual legs are omitted completely to prevent floor clipping bugs.
- Locomotion rejects all traditional VR movement standards. Joysticks and teleportation pointers are entirely disabled.
- Movement works by physical hand-to-surface collisions. Sphere colliders on the palm of the virtual hands interact directly with the game environment's mesh colliders.
- When a hand collider collides with a surface, the game calculates the real-world physical velocity vector of the controller's motion.
- It applies an equal and opposite force vector directly to the Rigidbody physics component of the Gorilla's main torso, propelling the player forward or upward.

3. ADVANCED MOVEMENT PHENOMENA (HIGH SKILL CEILING)
- Parallel Running: Staying flat to the ground and pushing horizontally to build maximum speed while keeping vertical bouncing minimal.
- Pinch Climbing: Wrapping both arms around an object, squeezing, and shoving straight down to pull the body straight up.
- Wall Climbing & Running: Using rapid, rhythmic angled shoves to bounce between two surfaces, or hitting a flat vertical wall parallel to stay suspended mid-air.
- Branching: Stringing parkour routes perfectly through tree networks by matching momentum vectors seamlessly from branch to branch.
- Verticaling / Lucying: Moving one or both hands in a hyper-fast circular motion against a flat vertical wall to climb straight up smoothly.

4. DIEGETIC DESIGN SYSTEM (ZERO INTERFACE HOVER)
- The game uses 100% diegetic user interfaces, meaning menus exist physically in the 3D environment rather than floating on a 2D screen.
- Gorilla OS Computer: Changing rooms, switching usernames, looking at ban details, or picking servers require physically hitting oversized keyboard keys inside the central map stump.
- The Organic Tutorial: New players drop into a deep cave upon launching the game. They must naturally learn to jump and climb out of the pit to gain access to the public game lobbies.
- The Cosmestic Mall: Buying items uses an in-game currency called "Shiny Rocks" spent in a virtual shop, where players physically look in mirrors and place hats directly onto their model.

5. AUDIO-VISUAL LOGISTICS
- Graphics: Utilizes low-poly models, unlit/flat shaders, and pixelated textures mimicking PlayStation 1 era aesthetics.
- Performance: The intentionally simple art style drastically minimizes draw calls, allowing mobile VR chipsets to run physics scripts at rock-solid framerates to mitigate motion sickness.
- Spatial Audio: Photon Voice mimics real-world acoustics. Sound volume drops off over distance, and audio panning changes depending on whether another player is standing above, below, or behind you.
